Full Form: Equated Monthly Instalment
Definition: A fixed amount paid monthly to repay a loan or finance a purchase over an agreed period.
Components: Each EMI comprises the principal amount and the interest charged on it.
Benefits: Helps manage large expenses by spreading repayment over time.
Calculation: EMI is calculated using a standard formula that considers the loan amount, interest rate, and repayment tenure.
Types: Fixed structure or reducing balance structure, depending on the product.
Usage: Commonly used for home loans, car loans, personal loans, and education loans.
Importance: Provides a structured repayment mechanism, making credit more practical and accessible
